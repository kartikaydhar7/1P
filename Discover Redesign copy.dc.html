Turn 3 · Discover · a search engine for prospects

Discover, reframed as Exa for go-to-market

At its core this is a place to find any company, contact, or signal in the world — by filters, signals or events — like "agricultural companies in Argentina that recently had a data breach." So the search is the hero. The Mission Control principles stay, wrapped around it: Paula turns your words into legible, editable criteria, every result carries a "because", queries start from a smart default rather than a blank box, and nothing hides how the match was made. Three takes on how much is a search bar vs. a builder vs. a research agent. Pick a badge (e.g. 3a).

Turn 2 · Discover · the opening moment · superseded

Three ways to open Discover

Today Discover opens on a blank box — "How can I help you today?" — so you start at zero and do all the work. These three apply the same principles as Mission Control: value before the ask (Paula has already searched), a smart default you review instead of author, never starting at 0%, one isolated hero action, and a "because" on every recommendation. Same Evergreen palette, same Paula. Only the shape of discovery differs. Pick one by badge (e.g. 2b) and I'll build it into v8.
