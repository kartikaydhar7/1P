Verdict

A confident agent-first foundation. The trust architecture around Paula — guardrails, approval gates, editable plans, visible reasoning — is genuinely strong and ahead of most agent products. What holds the prototype back is resilience (layout breaks at moderate widths), a handful of consistency drifts, and a persuasion layer that is mostly missing: defaults, progress, and loss-framing are incidental rather than designed.

The five moves with the highest leverage: fix the responsive floor (C1), dock Paula out of the content area (B2), give every automation a dry-run and an undo (B1), pre-fill segment creation instead of opening on a blank textarea (E1), and frame inaction as loss in the signal feed (E5).

A · Flows & information architecture

7.0/10

The sidebar reads as the funnel — capture (Website Visitors) → qualify (Discover, Prospects, Segments) → convert (Opportunities) → automate (Agents) — a strong spatial model, reinforced by the eyebrow taxonomy (WORKSPACE, PIPELINE, PLATFORM). Segment detail's five tabs map cleanly onto the object's lifecycle, and Discover's two modes (prompt hero ↔ results workspace) keep first contact simple. The weakness is object-model discipline: accounts and contacts appear in four different anatomies, and cross-tab state leaks.

A1
Major
Four modules present overlapping account lists with different anatomies

Evidence  Discover results, Prospects, segment-detail Contacts and Website Visitors each render account/contact tables with different columns, filters and row actions; there is no canonical account surface.

Fix  Define one account/contact row → drawer → full-profile pattern and reuse it everywhere; modules should differ by scope and filters, never by anatomy.

A2
Major
Prospects → Contacts keeps account-scoped state

Evidence  On the Contacts tab the saved views still read "All accounts 142 / Pricing-intent 41…", the result count says "486 accounts" for contact rows, and the header CTA remains "+ Add Account".

Fix  Scope saved views, counts and the primary CTA to the active tab ("486 contacts", "+ Add Contact").

A3
Minor
Mission Control KPIs are dead ends

The five stat cards (312 visitors, 142 accounts, $2.4M pipeline…) have no click affordance or drill-down. Make each one a link into the pre-filtered module view — the home page should be the shortest path to every number it shows.

A4
Minor
Discover's "VERSION 2 / 3" pager is unexplained

It sits under the query with no hint of what a version is. Label it ("Query drafts"), or fold revisions into the Recent Searches sidebar where history already lives.

A5
Minor
No navigation-stack rules for deep states

Workflow builder, segment detail and Notification Center each replace the canvas with only a single "back" affordance. Define what back returns to from every state (and what survives it) before build — retreat paths are where prototypes quietly break.

B · AI & agent interaction

8.0/10

The strongest area, and the product's real differentiation. The guardrails panel (daily send cap, approval-gated writes, working hours, exclusions) is a legible trust surface most agent products lack. Paula proposes and the user disposes: assembled workflows arrive as numbered, editable plans with per-step rationale. Scope is disclosed up front ("Paula reads your segments, signals & visitors"), Discover's thinking trace makes reasoning checkable, and AUTO vs APPROVAL chips make autonomy level a first-class property of every workflow. Two gaps keep it from excellent: recovery and spatial discipline.

B1
Major
No undo or rollback story after commitment

Evidence  "Set up this workflow" offers no dry-run, no preview of affected records, and no visible pause-and-revert once live. The send cap exists but is buried in segment settings.

Fix  Add a dry-run preview at setup ("would contact 41 accounts today") and a standard pause / undo affordance on every live automation. Reversibility is what makes AUTO mode adoptable.

B2
Major
The agent blocks the manual path

Evidence  The floating "Ask Paula" pill overlaps content on every screen — including the confirm button of Create Segment, where it literally covers the primary action.

Fix  Dock Paula into the shell (sidebar or top bar) or make the pill collision-aware. The agent must never occlude the hand-driven route to the same outcome.

B3
Minor
"ONLINE" presence is unexplained

What does an offline embedded agent mean? Either remove presence entirely or bind it to something real and diagnosable (connection health, degraded tools).

B4
Minor
The thinking trace only exists as "Complete"

No in-progress state is designed, yet the wait is where trust is won or lost. Design the running trace — steps checking off live, with the 2.1M-company scan as the moment of drama.

B5
Minor
"Voice Briefing" outranks a live agent error

In the notification drawer the loudest element is a full-width orange Voice Briefing CTA sitting above an AGENT ERROR card. Errors outrank novelty — demote the briefing until it has earned the slot.

C · Visual design & consistency

6.0/10

The warm paper palette and mono-for-data / Inter-for-UI pairing give the product a calm, distinctive voice that avoids dashboard-blue genericism, and the card language is applied consistently. The problems are structural resilience and a few rules that were never written down.

C1
Critical
Layout breaks at moderate viewport widths

Evidence  At ~920px every screen shows a horizontal scrollbar, and tab / button labels wrap to two lines: "Outreach Agent", "My Preferences", "Resume Workflow", "All accounts".

Fix  Set a responsive floor (e.g. 1024px), let tables own horizontal scroll internally, and give tabs, buttons and chips white-space:nowrap with min-widths. This is the first thing a laptop-with-sidebar demo will hit.

C2
Major
Primary-action color is inconsistent

Evidence  Primary is orange across the app, but Create Segment's "Generate" and confirm are green, and Discover's submit is near-black.

Fix  One primary (orange). Reserve green for success and health states, and keep black out of the action palette.

C3
Minor
Italic display words appear in some H1s only

"Website visitors" and "Agents & workflows" get the flourish; Discover, Segments and Opportunities don't. Apply it everywhere or drop it.

C4
Minor
Purple means "AI" — but the rule is unwritten

Paula's icon, the configuration assistant and MCP surfaces all use purple against the orange brand. It works — codify it: purple = agent surface, never decoration, so it survives the next ten screens.

C5
Minor
Stat-card anatomy drifts between modules

Value/label order, trend placement and casing vary across Mission Control, Segments, Opportunities and Connections. Write one KPI-card spec and apply it.

D · Copy & tone

7.0/10

Consistently good instincts: benefit-first subtitles under every H1 ("Reusable audiences that keep themselves current…"), concrete step rationale in workflows ("Keep ICP fit ≥ 80 with an active buying-intent signal; dedupe against CRM"), and notifications that lead with the noun that matters and always pair alert with action. The issues are hygiene and vocabulary discipline.

D1
Major
Persona mismatch across the shell

Evidence  The greeting says "Good Evening, Dhar" while the account chip reads ashwini.singh.

Fix  One persona, everywhere. Stakeholders notice this before they notice the design.

D2
Minor
Terminology blur: prospects / accounts / agents / workflows

"Prospects" contains Accounts + Contacts; "Agents & workflows" lists workflow cards while Paula is the agent. Write a glossary — agent = actor, workflow = recipe — and consider renaming Prospects to Accounts.

D3
Minor
Jargon lands unexplained at first contact

ICP fit ≥ 80, Deep Intel, De-anonymize, PPFOs. Add first-use tooltips or spell terms out on first instance per screen.

D4
Minor
Casing drifts across CTAs and eyebrows

"Build list from these" vs "Voice Briefing"; eyebrow taxonomy mixes WORKSPACE with CAPTURE · LEAD-GEN. Sentence case for actions, mono caps for eyebrows — then audit every string.

E · Behavioral principles

5.0/10

The team's own diagnosis — that the prototype isn't built on explicit behavioral principles — is broadly correct. The bones are strong, but persuasion is incidental: where a principle appears, it's a side effect of good sense rather than a designed system. Each of the six principles below gets a verdict and the single most concrete place to apply it next iteration.

E1
Partial
Smart defaults

Today  Genuinely good in places: NL queries auto-parse into editable filter chips, workflows arrive pre-scheduled ("Runs daily · 8:00am"), guardrails ship with sane values (cap 120, writes gated).

Apply  Create Segment opens on an empty textarea — the one place users must author from zero. Pre-fill a reviewable draft from the ICP and recent signals ("We drafted this from your ICP — adjust and go"), lift Quick Starts above the blank field, and default the name. Review-and-adjust beats compose-from-scratch.

E2
Weak
Goal gradient

Today  Only incidental instances (Enriched 89% of 780; committee 3/5). Nothing shows progress toward setup or launch, and nothing starts past zero.

Apply  A workspace setup meter that never reads 0 ("2 of 6 connections live"), progress on segment builds and enrichment runs, and a launch checklist on workflow setup with completed steps pre-checked (trigger ✓, guardrails ✓ — one step left).

E3
Partial
Reciprocity

Today  Website Visitors shows identified companies before asking to "Build list from these"; Discover returns results before any save ask. Value-first is half-built.

Apply  Extend it to first-run and empty states: show three pre-enriched sample accounts or a live visitor snapshot before asking to connect a CRM or configure anything. Give before you gate.

E4
Partial
IKEA effect

Today  Sourcing-criteria chips, outreach instructions and pre-launch Edit are all modifiable — but everything arrives fully assembled, so users never invest anything.

Apply  Add light co-authorship at moments of commitment: name the agent, pick tone and touch count, confirm guardrails during setup rather than in a buried tab. Default first, customize second — ownership without friction.

E5
Weak
Loss aversion

Today  "At risk" deal health and "12 contacts pending sync" hint at stakes; nothing frames the cost of inaction.

Apply  Frame time-decay explicitly: "41 pricing-intent accounts go cold in 6 days"; paused-workflow banners tallying what's slipping ("Paused 2h — 9 hot visitors not contacted"); digests framed as missed signals, not summaries.

E6
Weak
Contrast effect

Today  Absent — options are always presented one at a time, so nothing anchors the choice.

Apply  Present committing choices side by side with tradeoffs: AUTO vs APPROVAL modes, 4-touch vs 8-touch sequences, "Run once" vs "Runs daily" with expected outcomes — so the recommended option reads as the sensible middle, not a leap.

Method & limitations

Interactive heuristic walkthrough of the v6 standalone bundle (July 7, 2026; desktop, ~920–1280px) covering Mission Control, Discover, Prospects, Segments (list · detail · create), Website Visitors, Opportunities (board · table), Agents & Workflows, Connections, Personalization Engine, notifications (drawer · center · preferences) and the Paula workflow-assembly flow. Not exercised in this pass: the Discover account drawer → full-profile flow and the Live Monitor (present in project screenshots but not re-tested). This is expert review, not usability testing — scores reflect design maturity against best practice, not measured user success. All data shown in the prototype is mock.
